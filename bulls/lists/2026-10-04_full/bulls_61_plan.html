Ranking: by the conversion rate needed to reach 5x ROAS = 5 × CPC ÷ earnings per sale. Lower is better. Offers where only a % is known (order value unknown) show instead the order value needed for 5x if 3% of clicks buy.
Test budget: enough clicks to expect 5 sales if the offer really converts at the 5x level (≈ one payout), capped at $300. If it gets 0 sales by half the clicks, pause: an offer that truly converts at the 5x level gets 0 sales in that window only ~8% of the time.
digidip: on % offers digidip keeps 30%, so the rate used is 70% of the listed rate; flat payouts are used in full.
Scale only if: ROAS ≥ 5x with at least 3 confirmed sales. EUR→USD at 1.1225 (ECB, 2 Oct 2026). Nothing here has been launched.
