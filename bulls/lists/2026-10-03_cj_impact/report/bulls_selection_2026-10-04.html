Economics per click

"Break-even payout" = CPC ÷ CR: an offer must pay at least this per conversion to break even at that conversion rate. Revenue per click = real payout × CR. Margin per click = revenue per click − CPC. Break-even CR = CPC ÷ real payout: the conversion rate needed just to get our money back.
